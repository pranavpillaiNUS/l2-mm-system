\section{Locked Research Design}

\subsection{Price, microprice, and order-flow imbalance}

For best bid $b_t$, best ask $a_t$, and their displayed quantities $q^b_t,q^a_t$,
the arithmetic mid and microprice are
\begin{equation}
 m_t=\frac{b_t+a_t}{2},\qquad
 \mu_t=\frac{q^b_ta_t+q^a_tb_t}{q^b_t+q^a_t}.
\end{equation}
The microprice moves toward the ask when bid-side displayed quantity is larger.
Its deviation from mid is expressed in basis points. This is the project's
simple reference-price signal, not an optimal-control solution calibrated to
an arrival-intensity model.

Top-of-book order-flow imbalance (OFI) uses the event construction associated
with \citet{cont2014}:
\begin{align}
 e_n={}&\mathbf{1}_{b_n\ge b_{n-1}}q^b_n
 -\mathbf{1}_{b_n\le b_{n-1}}q^b_{n-1} \\
 &-\mathbf{1}_{a_n\le a_{n-1}}q^a_n
 +\mathbf{1}_{a_n\ge a_{n-1}}q^a_{n-1}.
\end{align}
One-second raw OFI sums these increments over the preceding second. Normalized
OFI divides the sum by mean displayed bid-plus-ask top depth over contributing
updates. The exact implementation is \code{src/analysis/ofi_signal.py}.
The prior literature motivates this observable. The BTCUSDT results here are
the project's own measurements.

On the one-second sampling grid, the population response at horizon $h$ is
\begin{equation}
 r_{t,h}=10^4\frac{m_{t+h}-m_t}{m_t}.
\end{equation}
Regression reports the signal coefficient, its HAC statistic, $R^2$, and
predicted drift for a one-standard-deviation signal change. The last quantity
makes effect size interpretable across signal scales.

For maker fill $i$ with direction $s_i\in\{-1,+1\}$, the conditional response is
\begin{equation}
 z_{i,h}=10^4s_i\frac{m_{i+h}-m_{i^-}}{m_{i^-}},
 \qquad x_i=s_i\,\mathrm{OFI}_{i^-}.
\end{equation}
Here $m_{i^-}$ and the OFI anchor are strictly before the fill. A book sample at
the fill millisecond is excluded because it may already include the move
associated with that fill. This response measures side-aligned future mid
movement. A separate transaction-price markout measures spread capture and is
not substituted into the gate. Maximum anchor staleness and permitted future
sample lag are each 1,000 ms.

\subsection{Prospective hypotheses and decision rule}

The repository protocol \code{v3_development_protocol_v1} was committed at
\ProtocolCommit. It predicted a positive pooled one-second OFI coefficient
and a selected 30-second conditional contrast below 1.0 bps at both queue
endpoints. This is a repository specification, not an external registration.

At each endpoint, population support requires a common coefficient sign in at
least 75\% of usable windows, pooled HAC $|t|\ge2$, and an absolute effect of
at least 0.05 bps per signal standard deviation. The historical sign-stability
rule admits either dominant sign. The positive pooled-coefficient hypothesis
is reported separately.

The conditional analysis fixes bucket edges $[-1,-0.25,0,0.25,1]$ for the
side-aligned normalized signal. It selects the most-populated negative bucket
and the most-populated nonnegative bucket in pooled buy/sell observations,
breaking count ties by the existing ascending bucket order. The separation is
\begin{equation}
 \Delta=\overline z_{\mathrm{selected\ nonnegative},30s}
       -\overline z_{\mathrm{selected\ negative},30s}.
\end{equation}
With at least 30 observations in each selected bucket, $\Delta\ge1.0$ bps
passes and a smaller contrast fails. Missing or thinner buckets are
inconclusive. The compatibility label \code{inconclusive_power} denotes a
minimum-count rule, not a power calculation. The selected pair need not be the
two extreme buckets, so the entire response curve must accompany the contrast.

Both endpoints must be supported for the combined screen to be supported.
Any blocked endpoint blocks the combined screen. Otherwise any inconclusive
endpoint makes it inconclusive. A five-second fallback is exploratory and
cannot replace this primary decision. Even a supported premise would require a
separately locked development candidate. It would not automatically authorize
holdout evaluation.

\subsection{Uncertainty and the unit of inference}

Baseline confidence intervals resample the 24 five-hour windows 10,000 times
with seed 7 and use two-sided percentile 95\% limits. Window resampling
preserves dependence within each observed cluster. Session- and matched-lot
intervals remain diagnostics because their independence assumptions are
stronger. Resampling practice follows the bootstrap framework
\citep{efron1979}. It does not make these mechanically selected market windows
a random sample of all regimes.

The population regression uses Newey--West standard errors
\citep{neweywest1987}, with the larger of the overlapping-return horizon lag
and sample-size lag rule, capped at $n-1$. This addresses the specified local
serial dependence. It does not produce uncertainty across independently
sampled market regimes. A window-clustered population-effect interval is not
part of the completed protocol.

V3 adds a diagnostic clustered interval for the conditional contrast. The
original pooled sample selects the two labels once. Every bootstrap draw
resamples all 24 windows with replacement, bringing all their fills and
retaining zero-count clusters. Each draw recomputes pooled fill-weighted means
for those fixed labels. There are 10,000 draws using Python \code{Random(7)}.
Any draw lacking a selected bucket would withhold the interval rather than be
silently discarded. Both endpoints have zero undefined draws.

This interval conditions on the original bucket selection, is not
selection-adjusted, assumes independent window clusters, and does not change
the gate. Its role is to show how the selected contrast varies across this
development panel, not to turn a failed heuristic screen into a universal
negative result.

\subsection{Holdout boundary}

The 12 late-May holdout windows are strategy-sealed. Their membership and
pre-strategy regime descriptors are public. No candidate has been evaluated
on them. The completed V3 gate blocks the proposed \code{OFIGatedMM} before
candidate or holdout strategy evaluation. A new hypothesis would require a new
development protocol and a locked candidate before one holdout evaluation.
Leaving the holdout sealed is the prescribed outcome of this experiment.
