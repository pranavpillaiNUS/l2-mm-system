\begin{abstract}
This report describes the complete research and engineering path of an offline
market-microstructure system. An educational hourly bar backtester supplied
interfaces and testing lessons, but its performance claims were demoted after
execution and annualization limitations were identified. The project then
recorded Binance spot depth and aggregate trades, reconstructed an exact L2
book, modeled passive orders and queue position, and studied the difference
between price prediction and fill-conditioned economics.

The corrected V3 experiment reruns the same 24 five-hour development windows
under explicit private-event scheduling and a post-response-gated snapshot
policy. Each window aggregates five independently reset hourly episodes.
The microprice baseline has mean net P\&L of \VThreeMeanZero{} and
\VThreeMeanOne{} USDT per window at zero and proportional queue-cancellation
credit, respectively. Normalized OFI retains a positive population association
at one second, with HAC $t=\VThreeOFITStat$ and
$R^2=\VThreeOFIRSquared$. The predeclared 30-second selected-bucket separation
is \VThreeContrastZero{} and \VThreeContrastOne{} bps, below the 1.0 bps
materiality screen at both endpoints. Diagnostic window-clustered intervals
cross zero. The study therefore blocks the proposed OFI-gated candidate and
preserves the strategy-sealed holdout; it does not establish that all OFI
strategies are ineffective.

A C++17 implementation uses checked scaled integers and reproduces the exact
Python Decimal book's canonical bytes and state-hash transcript.
The recorded-hour parity test covers \NativeRecordedStates{} states.
Preloaded book-update benchmarks show a \NativeRecordedSpeedup{}-fold ratio of
Python to C++ median duration on the recorded hour. Parser, execution, research
accounting, language-boundary costs, and full replay require separate measurement.
The contribution is a traceable combination of execution-aware research,
negative development evidence, a deterministic behavioral reference, and a
measured native implementation, with limitations and reproduction steps made
explicit.
\end{abstract}
